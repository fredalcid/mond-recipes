# Estimated ongoing infrastructure costs

Prices were checked on 16 November 2026 from the providers' public pricing pages and recent pricing guides. They change often and promotional prices depend on the term you choose, so **check each provider's page before you pay**. Taxes (GST/QST) are extra.

## At launch (small site)

| Service | What it does | Plan | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hostinger | Runs the website (Node.js web app) | **Business** web hosting (5 Node.js apps, 2 CPU, 3 GB RAM) | About **CA$5.59/month** on a 48-month prepaid term (about CA$268 up front), then renews at about **CA$23.69/month** |
| Supabase | Database, sign-in, file storage | **Pro** (one project, Micro compute included, Canada Central region) | **US$25/month** |
| Domain | mondrecipes.com (or your choice) | Free for the first year with Hostinger's Business plan | Then about **US$20/year** for a .com at Hostinger (cheaper at some other registrars) |
| Resend | Account and newsletter emails | **Free**: 3,000 emails/month, 100/day, 1 domain | **$0** |
| Cloudflare Turnstile | Bot check on forms | Free | **$0** |
| Google Analytics | Visit statistics (optional) | Free | **$0** |
| Google AdSense | Advertising (optional) | Free; pays you if approved | **$0** |
| Affiliate programs | Shop commissions | Free to join | **$0** |

**Typical monthly total at launch: about US$25 (Supabase) + CA$5.59–23.69 (Hostinger), plus the domain once a year.**

The 48-month Hostinger price is the cheapest per month but is paid up front. Shorter terms cost more per month.

## When the site grows

| When | What to change | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| More than 100 emails a day (sign-up confirmations, password resets and newsletters together) | Resend **Pro**: 50,000 emails/month, no daily limit | **US$20/month** |
| The newsletter list grows | Resend's marketing plans are priced by number of contacts | See resend.com/pricing |
| The site feels slow at busy times | Hostinger **Cloud Startup** (4 CPU, 4 GB RAM) | About **CA$11.19/month** on 48 months, renews about **CA$36.29/month** |
| The database needs more power | A bigger Supabase compute size | Small adds about US$5/month after the included credit; larger sizes cost more |
| Database over 8 GB | Extra database disk | About US$0.125 per GB per month |
| Lots of photo and video traffic | Supabase includes 250 GB of data transfer a month; more is billed per GB | About US$0.09 per GB |
| Many members | Supabase Pro includes 100,000 monthly active users | Extra users are billed per user |
| Video becomes popular | A video service such as Cloudflare Stream or Mux (not built in) | Usage-based |

Supabase Pro has a **spend cap turned on by default**, so usage charges can't grow beyond the plan without you switching it off.

## Not needed

No payment processor, no checkout, no AI service and no paid search service: the Shop sends visitors to outside retailers, and search runs inside the database.

## One-time costs to consider

- A lawyer's review of the Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookie Policy and Affiliate Disclosure (Quebec law requires a privacy policy and a person in charge of personal information).
- A high-resolution version of the logo (PNG or SVG) for print and large banners.
- Licensed photos, if you don't take your own.
